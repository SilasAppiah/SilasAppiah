## Step 2. Set business hours (5 minutes)

Away messages only work if Meta knows your hours.

1. Open **Meta Business Suite** on a computer.
2. Go to **Inbox**, then **Automations** (top right).
3. Open **Away message** and set the schedule to **Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 17:00**, Ghana time (GMT). If Meta asks for Page business hours instead, set them in **Settings > Page info > Hours**.

Meta moves these menus around. If a name is slightly different on your screen, look for the closest match.

All of these are in **Inbox > Automations**. Set each one up for **both Facebook and Instagram**. Where a message contains a WhatsApp link, use the Facebook link in the Facebook version and the Instagram link in the Instagram version. That's how we'll know which platform the lead came from.

Create it under **Automations > Create automation > Comment to message**. If you can't find it, skip to the fallback below.

- **Keywords:** `PHRI`, `APHRI`, `SPHRI`, `DATES`, `FEES` plus lower-case versions if there's room (guides say up to 10).
- **Public reply:** `Thanks! We've sent you the details in your DMs.`

  > Hi! Thanks for your comment. Which certification would you like details on: aPHRi, PHRi or SPHRi? Reply with the name and we'll send fees, dates and how to apply.

**Fallback if it isn't available:** go to **Inbox > Facebook comments** or **Instagram comments**, open the comment and choose to send a **private message** using a saved reply (3f). Meta allows one private reply per comment, within 7 days.

**Post captions:** end posts with "Comment PHRI for dates and fees" (or APHRI or SPHRI), written exactly as the keyword.

## Step 5. Tag every website link (10 minutes)

Use the **UTM builder** sheet in the tracker for any link to the website or portal. Google Analytics will then show visits by source.

Naming rules, so reports stay clean (always lower case):
- `utm_source`: facebook, instagram, linkedin
- `utm_medium`: social (free posts) or paid_social (ads)
- `utm_campaign`: cohort49
- `utm_content`: programme and post type, for example `phri_classroom` or `aphri_testimonial`

Find the results in **Google Analytics > Reports > Acquisition > Traffic acquisition**, then change the dimension to **Session campaign** or **Session source/medium**.

>
> The code tells you where they found us: FB = Facebook, IG = Instagram, LI = LinkedIn. The second part is the programme. GEN means a general enquiry and CONSULT means HRCC Consulting.

> This helps us show which posts bring real enrolments, so we can do more of what works. Thank you!

**To create the label in WhatsApp Business:** open a chat, tap the contact name or the menu, choose **Label chat**, then **New label**, name it **Social lead**, and save.

## Step 7. Take the baseline this week (30 minutes)

Do this before the automations change anything, so the December comparison is fair.

1. In the Meta inbox, scroll back to the last 20 DMs that asked about a programme.
2. For each one, note when it arrived and when someone first replied personally (not an automated reply).
3. Enter them in the **Baseline DMs** sheet. It works out the average reply time and the share answered within 15 minutes.
4. For one week, jot down roughly how long you spend on repeat replies each day. Put the total in the weekly log.

## Step 9. The Friday routine (10 minutes)

1. Get the two numbers from Sales and enter them in the **Weekly log**.
2. From Meta Business Suite, count the week's enquiries and how many were fully answered by automation.
3. Add your hours on repeat replies.
4. Any question the automations couldn't answer? Write an answer and add it as a saved reply or keyword.
5. Any good question? Save it for next weekend's facilitator interviews (Innovation 1).

The **Dashboard** sheet updates itself and gives you the numbers for your monthly report.
