## The story in one line

**Capture the teaching, share it as value, and track who it brings in.**

Every weekend, three modules of expert teaching happen at HRCC and almost none of it reaches our audience. Innovation 1 turns that teaching into a steady flow of value content. Innovation 2 makes sure everyone that content attracts gets an instant answer and reaches sales with a tag we can count.

```
Facilitator interview  ->  Week of value posts  ->  Keyword comment / DM
        ^                                                   |
        |                                                   v
 New questions feed                               Instant reply + FAQ
 next interviews     <-  Sales enrols  <-  Tagged WhatsApp link (ref code)
```

## Innovation 1: Classroom to Content

### 1. The innovation
A 3-minute interview with the facilitator after every module, turned into a week of value content with the help of AI. It runs as a named series, "Straight from the Classroom".

### 2. Current challenge or gap
- Most content depends on the creative team, who are stretched across many requests.
- The weekly Wednesday reel shoot runs long because content is decided in the room. It pulls me away from my own tasks and I miss timelines.
- The feed carries both HRCC and Consulting programmes and feels salesy.
- Meanwhile, three modules of expert teaching happen every weekend and none of it is shared. Prospects cannot see what an HRCC class is actually like.

### 3. Objectives (by 31 December)
- Capture at least 80% of modules taught.
- Produce at least 4 posts from each interview.
- At least 50% of weekly posts made without the creative team.
- Cut the Wednesday shoot from [X] minutes to [Y] minutes.
- Keep posts scheduled at least 7 days ahead.

### 4. Action plan
1. **Saturday (in-person):** phone on a small tripod with a clip-on mic. Three minutes with the facilitator straight after class. I film, or a programme officer or class rep films using a one-page guide.
2. **Sunday (online):** the facilitator stays on the call for 3 to 5 minutes after participants leave. I join remotely, ask the questions and record. No participants are on camera.
3. **The same three questions every time:**
   - What is one idea from today an HR professional should use on Monday?
   - What mistake do you see people make on this?
   - What did a participant ask today that stuck with you?
4. **Monday (about 1 hour for all three):** auto-captions in CapCut with one saved template. Paste the transcript into an AI assistant to draft a reel caption, a LinkedIn post and 2 to 3 quote lines. Drop quotes into one reusable Figma quote-card template.
5. **Schedule Tuesday to Sunday:** Facebook and Instagram in Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn in Buffer (free plan: 3 channels, up to 10 queued posts each).
6. Every post ends with a keyword call to action ("Comment PHRI to get the next intake dates"), which feeds Innovation 2.
7. The Wednesday shoot now covers only what the interviews do not (announcements, promotions), so it gets shorter.

### 5. Resources and support required
- Executive Director's endorsement of the series.
- Facilitators' agreement (they are willing) and a one-line written consent from each.
- Programme officer or class rep to film on Saturdays when I cannot attend.
- One phone tripod and one clip-on mic (low cost, [GHS amount]).
- Free tools we already have or can use: CapCut, an AI assistant, Meta Business Suite, Buffer (free), Figma.

### 6. Timeline
- **October:** agree the format with facilitators, get consent, buy tripod and mic, build the CapCut and Figma templates. Record baselines (Wednesday shoot length, share of posts from the creative team, engagement on recent promotional posts). Pilot on two weekends and adjust the questions.
- **November:** full run every weekend, three interviews a week, four or more posts each. Track KPIs weekly.
- **December:** review which formats perform best. Compile the best clips into a "Cohort 49 highlights" set for Cohort 50 recruitment. Write a one-page playbook so anyone can run the routine.

## Innovation 2: Social Enquiry Routing

### 1. The innovation
Every social media enquiry gets an instant, accurate first answer through Meta Business Suite automations, backed by an AI-drafted FAQ library. Anyone ready to enrol is passed to sales through a programme-specific WhatsApp link that carries a reference code, so every handoff can be counted.

### 2. Current challenge or gap
- I answer social media DMs and comments by hand. The same questions come up again and again.
- The handoff to sales is a bare WhatsApp number. Nobody knows how many people actually message sales, which programme they want, or how many enrolments social media produces.
- Sales starts each conversation cold and often asks the person to repeat themselves.

### 3. Objectives (by 31 December)
- Instant first reply to every DM, and a personal reply within 15 minutes in working hours.
- At least 60% of routine questions answered by the FAQ library and automations.
- 100% of handoffs to sales go through a ref-coded link.
- A weekly count of social-sourced leads, shared with sales.
- Cut time on repetitive replies by [X] hours a week.

### 4. Action plan
1. Review the last two months of DMs and comments. List the top 15 questions.
2. Use AI to draft answers for each programme (aPHRi, PHRi, SPHRi) and for Consulting. Sales confirms the facts (fees, dates, requirements).
3. Set up in Meta Business Suite (free): instant reply, FAQs, saved replies, away message, custom keywords (up to 5, for example APHRI, PHRI, SPHRI, FEES, DATES) and comment-to-message on posts.
4. Create one WhatsApp click-to-chat link per programme with a pre-filled message, for example: "Hi, I'm interested in PHRi (ref: SM-PHRI)". The person taps, WhatsApp opens with the message ready, they press send.
5. Brief sales on one step: add the label "Social lead" in WhatsApp Business when a message has a ref code. Agree a quick Friday count.
6. Weekly 10-minute review: any question the FAQ missed becomes a new answer, and good questions become prompts for next weekend's facilitator interviews.

### 5. Resources and support required
- Executive Director's approval of the response standard and of the sales labelling step.
- Sales team: one label per tagged chat and a weekly count.
- Sales or programme team to sign off FAQ facts.
- Tools: Meta Business Suite and WhatsApp Business (both free, already in use).

### 6. Timeline
- **October:** capture baselines (time 20 recent DMs from message to reply, count weekly enquiries). Build the FAQ library, switch on automations, create the ref-coded links, brief sales.
- **November:** live on all Cohort 49 posts and the "Straight from the Classroom" series. Weekly counts with sales. Fix weak answers.
- **December:** compare against baselines. Report social-sourced leads and enrolments. Write the playbook and set up for Cohort 50.

### 7. KPIs

|---|---|---|
| Personal first reply time (working hours) | [X] min | 15 min or less |

### 8. Expected Q4 impact
- For the first time, HRCC can say how many leads and enrolments social media produced.

### 9. Sustainability

## Questions to prepare for

- **"Are we giving away paid content for free?"** No. One takeaway per module, not the lesson. It is the trailer, not the movie.
- **"Won't automated replies feel robotic?"** Only the first reply is automatic. A person takes over straight after. Answers are short and warm.
- **"What if people delete the pre-filled text?"** Some will, so the count is a minimum. Today we have no count at all.
- **"Isn't WhatsApp sales' job?"** Yes, and it stays theirs. My part ends at a clean, tagged handoff. Sales adds one label.
- **"What about data protection?"** The customer starts the WhatsApp chat, so HRCC is not sending unsolicited marketing. Facilitators give written consent and participants are not filmed.
- **"How will you prove before and after?"** Baselines are taken in the first week of October.
