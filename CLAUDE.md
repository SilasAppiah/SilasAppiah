## 5. Innovation 2: draft (The Connected Journey, Automated)

**1. The innovation [Proposed].** One connected path from first click to enrolment, where each step triggers the next without manual effort. Four parts:
- Scheduling: Buffer and Meta Business Suite queue content at least a week ahead, fed by the shortened Wednesday shoots.
- Instant response: auto-replies, comment-to-DM prompts (for example "comment INFO"), and WhatsApp Business greetings, quick replies and labels.
- Application recovery: people who start but do not finish an application get a short, friendly follow-up at day 1, day 3 and day 7.
- Community nurture: separate WhatsApp lists or a WhatsApp Channel per programme and per brand, so HRCC and Consulting audiences each get what is relevant.

**2. Gap.** After content goes out, follow-up depends on whether Silas has a free minute. Enquiries wait, unfinished applications are not chased on a set schedule, and the community goes quiet between cohorts. Selling also happens in public on the feed, which adds to the salesy feel. Moving the sales conversation into private channels frees the feed for value content.

**3. Objectives (targets proposed, confirm after baseline).**
- First response within 15 minutes in working hours.
- Every unfinished application followed up on the fixed schedule.
- Routine posts scheduled at least 7 days ahead.
- Less weekly time spent on repetitive replies and posting.

**4. Action plan.** Map the journey (social, enquiry, application, enrolment, alumni). Build an FAQ and quick-reply library for each programme and Consulting. Set up WhatsApp labels, greeting and away messages, and comment-to-DM prompts. Write three follow-up messages for unfinished applications. Spend 10 minutes a day checking the portal for started-but-unfinished applications and sending the matching message. Prepare scripts before each Wednesday shoot so it runs short and feeds the scheduler.

**5. Resources and support.** Executive Director approval of the response standard. Portal access to view or export started applications, and whoever manages the portal to confirm what is possible. A named backup to cover the inbox when Silas is away. A low-cost automation tool only if the manual version works first.

**6. Timeline.**
- October: capture baselines, journey map, message library, switch on auto-replies and labels.
- November: launch application follow-up and comment-to-DM for the Cohort 49 campaign, set up segmented lists.
- December: review results, improve the weakest messages, write the playbook, prepare the Q1 handover.

**7. KPIs.** First response time, % of enquiries answered within target, application completion rate (completed divided by started), number of applications recovered, % of posts scheduled 7 or more days ahead, weekly hours on routine replies, missed deadlines.

**8. Expected Q4 impact.** Illustrative maths only: if 100 applications start a month and 30 are left unfinished, recovering 1 in 5 gives 6 extra completed applications a month. Replace with Silas's real portal numbers before it goes on a slide.

**9. Sustainability.** The message library, labels and schedule become a written playbook a backup can run. Results go into Silas's monthly performance report. The same system can extend to other programmes and to HRCC Consulting.

**[Verify] before this goes on a slide:**
